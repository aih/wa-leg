II. A - Brief Description Of What The Measure Does That Has Fiscal Impact

Significant provisions of the bill and any related workload or policy assumptions that have revenue or expenditure impact on the responding agency by section number.

CURRENT LAW:

{{fee.collector}} collect a {{fee.currentAmount}} {{fee.name}} on {{fee.base}}. {{narrative.currentLaw.deposit}}

PROPOSAL:

This bill increases the {{fee.name}} from {{fee.currentAmount}} to {{fee.newAmount}} per {{fee.unit}}. {{narrative.proposal.deposit}}

Beginning {{fee.indexStartDate}}, and every {{fee.indexIntervalYears}}th year thereafter, the Department of Revenue (department) must adjust and publish the {{fee.name}} amount to reflect the growth of the consumer price index. The department must round the adjusted amount to the nearest {{fee.rounding}} and cannot change it once the adjustment takes effect. The most recent fee amount becomes the base for future adjustments.

Consumer price index (CPI-U) means the most current seasonally adjusted index for the {{fee.cpiSeries}} expenditure category of the consumer price index for all urban consumers, as published by {{fee.cpiPublishDate}} by the federal Bureau of Labor Statistics.
