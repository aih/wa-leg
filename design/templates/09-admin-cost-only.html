FIRST YEAR COSTS:

The department will incur total costs of {{expenditures.total.fy1}} in fiscal year {{fy.1.year}}. These costs include:

Labor Costs – Time and effort equate to {{fte.total.fy1}} FTEs.

  • Amend one administrative rule.
  • Set up, program, and test computer system changes.
  • Create a special notice and update relevant information on the department’s website.
  • Gathering requirements, attending implementation meetings, and documenting and testing system changes.
  • Process tax return work items, assist taxpayers with reporting questions, and respond to inquiries via email, web message, and paper correspondence.
  • Examine accounts and make corrections as necessary.
  • Respond to data requests and questions, compile statistics, and manage data.
  • Answer additional phone calls and counter inquiries from businesses, individuals, and accountants regarding tax questions and tax return preparation.

Object Costs - {{objects.nonlabor.fy1}}.

  • Computer system changes, including contract programming.
  • Printing and postage
